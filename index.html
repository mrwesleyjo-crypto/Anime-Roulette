Multiverse Anime Tournament

Goal: draft a roster of anime fighters, then spin your way through 6 tournament leagues. Beat every opponent in a league's bracket to win that league's Trophy and unlock the next one. Clear the Multiverse Final to become the undisputed Champion.

How it works
  • Scout — spin to recruit a new fighter to your roster (rarer fighters are stronger, but less likely).
  • Train — spin to pick a roster member and permanently boost their power.
  • Trade — spin to swap a random roster member for a freshly scouted one.
  • Match — fight the next opponent in your current league's bracket, shown on the map below.
