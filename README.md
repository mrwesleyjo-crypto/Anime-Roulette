🌀 Multiverse Anime Tournament

Draft your dream anime roster. Spin your way through six brutal leagues. Become the undisputed Champion of the Multiverse.

A free, browser-based gacha-roulette tournament game featuring 95+ characters pulled from dozens of your favorite anime — Naruto, One Piece, Dragon Ball, Bleach, Demon Slayer, Jujutsu Kaisen, My Hero Academia, Hunter x Hunter, and way, way more — all thrown into one chaotic multiverse to fight it out.

No sign-up. No download. No ads. Just open it and spin.

🎮 What is this, exactly?

Every action in this game — recruiting fighters, training them, fighting opponents, even trading — happens on a slot-machine roulette. You draft a starting fighter, then spin your way through a gauntlet of six escalating leagues (Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Diamond → Multiverse Final), facing off against 24 real anime villains along the way. Lose a match and your run ends... unless something saves you.

It's part gacha collector, part roguelike gambler, part anime fan-service — and it takes maybe 15–20 minutes to play through a full run.

✨ Why you'll want to keep spinning
🎰 Six rarity tiers, from Useless (yes, some characters are canonically terrible fighters — looking at you, Yamcha) all the way up to Mythic. Power levels go full Dragon Ball — expect numbers well over 9000.
🔥 Awakening system — awaken key fighters through multiple transformation phases mid-run. Watch Naruto reach Six Paths Sage Mode, Goku hit Ultra Instinct, or Luffy unlock Gear Fifth — complete with a dedicated, screen-shaking, sound-blasting reveal for every single transformation.
⚡ Special fusions — get Goku and Vegeta on the same roster and the multiverse won't let you not fuse them into Gogeta. Several other legendary pairings are waiting to be discovered.
🧬 100+ team synergies — some are just for flavor, some are genuinely powerful, and a few are actively bad for you (put too many "chosen one" protagonists on one team and watch the ego clashes tank your odds).
🎁 Real risk, real tension — a dynamic, suspense-scaled reveal system means every pull feels like a pull, whether you land a Common or something absurd. The odds are never as obvious as they look.
📖 A living Collection Index, career-wide achievements, daily quests, a shard-based shop, and a local leaderboard to chase your own best runs.
🃏 Downloadable victory badge — win or lose, generate a shareable image of your final roster to flex (or commiserate) with.
📱 Actually built for mobile — a persistent roster bar keeps your team visible while you spin, no matter how deep you've scrolled.
🥚 Secrets. There's more hiding in here than we're going to spoil in a README. Some pulls are rarer than others. Some defeats aren't really defeats. Some characters shouldn't exist in this game at all. Good luck.
🚀 Playing it

Just visit the site — nothing to install, nothing to configure. Draft your first fighter, hit spin, and see where the multiverse takes you.

The basics:

Action	What it does
🥷 Scout	Spin to recruit a new fighter — rarer ones are stronger, but far less likely
🥋 Train	Permanently boost a roster member's power
🌌 Dimensional Swap	Pull a fighter through a rift and see who it trades places with
⚔️ Match	Face the next opponent in your league's bracket
🎁 Treasure	Find a one-time support item
💥 Awakening	Unlock a fighter's next transformation (Gold League onward)

Lose a match and you're eliminated — unless your Phoenix Ember saves you first.

🛠️ Built with

Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No frameworks, no build step, no dependencies — just three files (index.html, style.css, script.js) and a folder of character art. Runs entirely in your browser, with all progress saved locally.
